## Who I am
T cell immunologist.

## Core interests (rank these highest)
- TCR specificity: how TCRs recognise peptide-MHC, antigen discovery, specificity prediction.
- TCR cross-reactivity: molecular mimicry, one TCR recognising several peptides.
- TCR-pMHC structure: crystal and cryo-EM structures, docking geometry, structure prediction.
- New methods in the T cell field, experimental (screens, TCR sequencing, pMHC libraries,
  single-cell) or computational (ML for TCR-pMHC binding, repertoire analysis, structure modelling).

## Niche interest
- EBV and multiple sclerosis, especially CD8 T cells: EBV-specific CD8s in MS,
  cross-reactivity between EBV and CNS antigens, B cell/EBV-directed therapies and their effect on T cells.

## Always show me
- The big immunology stories of the week, whatever the topic (major papers in
  Nature, Science, Cell, Immunity, Nature Immunology, Science Immunology).

## Leave out
- MS papers without an EBV or T cell angle (routine clinical, imaging, DMT outcome studies).
- Small clinical studies measuring antibodies or markers in a handful of patients.
- Case reports, reviews that add nothing new, CAR-T manufacturing/clinical trial updates
  unless they reveal something about TCR biology.
